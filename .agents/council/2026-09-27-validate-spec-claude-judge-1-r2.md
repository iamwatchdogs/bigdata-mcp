## R2 extended analysis

### 1. Restatement of my R1 position

I returned **WARN / HIGH**. My reasoning was not that the design is unsound but that the design is
**not yet self-consistent on the axis it claims to guarantee**. Nearly every MCP protocol and SDK
claim I tested verified TRUE against primary sources — including the two I was sent to attack,
`ctx.elicit()` failing outright on 2026-07-28 and `Resolve(...)` being the era-portable replacement.
But the mandated low-level `Server` tier silently strips the `requestState` integrity protection
that the spec's own OIDC/MRTR design depends on, contradicting its structural guarantees; and
`httpx` is both designated (§3/§4) and rejected (§19). I also flagged a missing `resultType`
wire-frame note and an absent MRTR retry conformance row.

**Nothing in R2 revises any of that.** I re-read the migration guide and low-level server docs and
found *more* support for the same family: the low-level tier also stopped converting raised
exceptions into `isError: true` results, so mandate 1 invalidates a second normative table
independently (f-j1-r2-004). The R2 finding that would have moved me is different in kind, and it is
new: the OIDC flow is built on a mechanism the SDK documents as legacy-incapable.

### 2. What I got right, and got right for the wrong reason

Two claims I flagged or worried about in R1 are confirmed, and I want to record the corrections
precisely because a self-correction that only runs one way is not a self-correction.

- **URL-mode elicitation IS expressible as MRTR.** Before writing f-j1-r2-001 I assumed the
  capability gate and the absence of a URL resolver might make §15.2 unimplementable. It is not:
  *"URL-mode elicitation rides this exact mechanism on a 2026 connection. The entry in
  `input_requests` is an `ElicitRequest` whose params are `ElicitRequestURLParams`; the user
  finishes the out-of-band flow and your client retries the call. Same loop, no new API."* The
  finding I filed is therefore about the *stated failure behaviour* and the *unstated capability
  precondition* — not about the mechanism being unavailable. That is a materially smaller claim
  than the one I set out to file, and the smaller one is the true one.
- **`ctx.elicit_url()` is a real API.** §6.2 lists it among the deprecated calls. I checked for it
  expecting a hallucinated method name; it exists on `ServerSession` and is covered by the
  `NoBackChannelError` rule alongside `ctx.elicit()`. §6.2 is correct. No finding.
- **`Client(..., input_required_max_rounds=10)` and the SDK's 600s per-round seal are reasonable**,
  not hostile. I had predicted the round cap would create a busy-poll hazard; it does, but the
  server controls whether it returns bare `request_state`, so the hazard is opt-in (f-j1-r2-003).
